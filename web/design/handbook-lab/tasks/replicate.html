Keep more than one copy

Which copy can accept writes when something fails?

Follow a change from writer to replica, then work through lag and failure. Keep data movement, durability and failover decisions separate.

Where they differ Quorum and promotion rules differ. MongoDB sharding distributes data; it is not another name for replication.
