08 / The database path

Protect it and change it safely

What has to remain true through the change?

Make access and transport explicit. Rehearse upgrades and migrations, validate the destination, and know when a rollback would lose new writes.

Where they differ Encryption features, upgrade paths and migration tools are product-specific. Keep the exact version and edition beside the procedure.
