06 / The database path

Back up, then restore

Can you get the right data back?

Decide how much loss and downtime you can tolerate. Choose a backup method, restore somewhere isolated and check the recovered data—not just the command's exit code.

Where they differ Logical dumps, physical copies and log replay have different consistency and version constraints. Replication is not a substitute for backup.
